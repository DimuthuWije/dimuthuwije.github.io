I am a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) graduate in **Electrical and Electronics Engineering** from <a href="https://www.curtin.edu.au/">Curtin University</a>, focused on robotics and autonomous systems, particularly on developing intelligent mobile robots capable of operating in complex and unstructured environments. My previous work has included mobile robotic platforms, computer vision-based perception systems, embedded systems and hardware development for real-world applications.

My current research interests focus on the intersection of autonomous mobile robotics, robot perception, embedded systems & intelligent control. I'm interested in autonomous navigation, sensor integration & robotic systems for challenging environments.

I previously worked as an **Undergraduate Research Assistant** at <a href="https://scorelab.org">SCoRe Lab</a>, **University of Colombo School of Computing** and as a **Graduate Research Assistant** at the <a href="https://www.sjp.ac.lk/">University of Sri Jayewardenepura</a>, contributing to robotics, embedded systems, sensing technologies and applied engineering research.

As an **Application Engineer** at <a href="https://www.synopsys.com">Synopsys</a>, I contribute to semiconductor verification solutions while continuing to develop my robotics portfolio through research projects, engineering projects and publications.

## Research Interests

- Autonomous Mobile Robotics
- Robot Perception & Sensor Integration
- Navigation in Unstructured Environments
- Embedded Robotic Systems
- Intelligent Robot Control
